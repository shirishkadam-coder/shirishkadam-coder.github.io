Using data to solve real problems

I'm a final-year student of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Ajeenkya DY Patil School of Engineering, Lohegaon, Pune. I like projects that start with a real problem: people without shelter in our cities, small businesses that can't see their own numbers, and waste that gets mixed up because no one sorts it.

Most of my work sits between machine learning and data analytics. I collect and clean the data, train the model, and build the dashboard or app that puts the result in front of people. I recently completed a Data Science internship focused on Power BI.
